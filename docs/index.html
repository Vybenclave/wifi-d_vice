WIFI D_VICE — Interface Simulator

A click-through simulation of the real device's touchscreen firmware, running in your browser with fabricated sample data standing in for live radio scans. Every screen and menu is here — tap around exactly like you would on the hardware. Source: github.com/Vybenclave/wifi-d_vice

What you're looking at

This is a UI simulation, not the firmware. Every screen, menu, and setting is real and navigable, but WiFi/BLE/sub-GHz data is generated locally to demonstrate what a session looks like — nothing here scans real airspace. Alerts, detections and drift happen on a timer so the device feels alive as you explore it.
